Perfume Notes & Structure

Every fragrance is layered into three essential notes that unfold smoothly over time.

Layer 01

Top Notes

First Impression (0 - 15 mins)

The light, vibrant scent you perceive immediately upon application. Features citrus, bergamot, and light herbs.

Layer 02

Heart / Middle Notes

The Core (15 mins - 4 hours)

The main personality of the perfume that forms once the top notes fade. Filled with floral, fruity, or spicy elements.

Layer 03

Base Notes

The Trail (4+ hours)

Deep, long-lasting ingredients that anchor the fragrance. Features rich woods, amber, musk, vanilla, and oud.
